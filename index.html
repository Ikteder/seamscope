The method

One intelligent
pixel path at a time.

  1. 01

    Measure contrast

    A Sobel operator scores local luminance change while strong boundary energy protects the outer frame.

  2. 02

    Accumulate cost

    Dynamic programming finds the cheapest connected path across the entire image, including your brush bias.

  3. 03

    Remove and repeat

    Pixels shift by one position, the energy field is recomputed, and the next seam adapts to the new image.
